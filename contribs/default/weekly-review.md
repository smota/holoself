# Weekly Review — Session Structure

A lighter session format for end-of-week or start-of-week check-ins.
Use instead of the full Mochary session when you want a pulse, not a deep coaching session.
Runs 20–30 minutes.

---

## When to Use

- End of Friday or start of Monday
- No major issue to work through — maintenance, not intervention
- After a heavy week when depth is less available
- When the user asks for a "quick check-in" or "weekly review"

---

## Structure

### 1. Wins (5 min)
Ask: "What moved forward this week?"

Listen for specifics. If they list abstract things ("I worked hard"), push: "What did you
complete or ship?" The goal is to make visible what was real — wins are often invisible
to the person living them.

Do not rush past this. It anchors the session in fact before moving into what did not happen.

---

### 2. Misses (5 min)
Ask: "What didn't happen that you wanted to happen?"

State it plainly. One follow-up question: "What happened?" Not "why" — just what.

If the same miss appears for a second week running, name the pattern: "This is the second
week [X] didn't happen. What's going on there?" Then move on. Do not dwell.

---

### 3. Top Priority (5 min)
Ask: "What's the one thing that matters most next week?"

Press for one. If they give three: "If only one of those happened and made the rest feel
secondary, which one is it?"

Then: "When specifically are you working on it? What block on your calendar?"

---

### 4. One Thing to Avoid (5 min)
Ask: "What habit or behavior do you most want to avoid next week?"

This surfaces the behavioral pattern — the thing they know they do and want to not repeat.
Naming it as a pre-commitment is more effective than discovering it after the fact.

---

### 5. Energy Check (5 min)
Ask: "How's your energy going into next week? Body — not assessment."

Use the answer to calibrate realism. Low energy: protect the top priority, cut optional
commitments. High energy: where can they stretch into something they have been avoiding?

---

## Post-Session

Save brief notes: wins, misses, top priority, one-thing-to-avoid, energy level.
These feed the accountability review at the next full session.
