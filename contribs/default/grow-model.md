# GROW Model — Session Structure

Focused coaching conversation in four phases. Use as an alternative to the Mochary sequential
format when the user brings a specific goal or decision to work on.

Origin: Whitmore, Gallwey. Widely applied in business coaching.

---

## When to Use GROW Over Mochary

| Situation | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| User has one specific decision or problem today | GROW |
| Ongoing accountability across many areas | Mochary |
| User is stuck on a single thing | GROW |
| Recurring weekly session covering the full picture | Mochary |
| User needs to explore options broadly | GROW |

GROW and Mochary are complementary. You can run a GROW sequence inside a Mochary step
when a single issue needs depth before moving on.

---

## The Four Phases

### G — Goal
What does the person want from *this conversation*? Not life. This session.

**Key questions:**
- "What outcome do you want from our time today?"
- "How will you know if this was useful?"
- "If this conversation goes well, what's different afterward?"

Pin a specific goal before moving. "Clarity on X" is a goal. "Feel better" is not.

---

### R — Reality
What is the current situation, honestly stated?

**Key questions:**
- "What's happening right now?"
- "What have you already tried?"
- "What's the gap between where you are and where you want to be?"
- "What's making this hard?"

No editorializing. Draw out facts. The user often knows the answer — they need to hear
themselves say it. Do not rush to options.

---

### O — Options
What could they do? Explore broadly before converging.

**Key questions:**
- "What are your options?"
- "What else could you do?" (Ask 3–4 times — AWE from Bungay Stanier)
- "What would you do if [the main constraint] weren't there?"
- "What would someone you respect do here?"
- "What's the option you haven't mentioned yet?"

Do not propose options until the user is exhausted. The unmentioned option is often the real one.

---

### W — Will / Way Forward
What will they actually do?

**Key questions:**
- "What are you going to do?"
- "When exactly?"
- "What could stop you — and how will you handle that?"
- "On a scale of 1–10, how committed are you? What would make it a 10?"

Convert options to commitments. Commitment below 7/10 is not a commitment.
Apply Impeccable Agreements: precisely defined, timebound, stated as fact not intention.
