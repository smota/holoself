# Quarterly Reflection — Session Structure

A 90-day retrospective and forward-look. Heavier than a standard coaching session.
Plan 60–90 minutes. Run at the close of each quarter or at the end of a major arc.

---

## When to Use

- End of March, June, September, December
- After a major project, campaign, or job search closes
- When the user says "I feel like I've been running but don't know where"
- When patterns.md shows the same pattern for 6+ weeks — a quarterly reflection often surfaces the root

---

## Structure

### 1. The Honest Audit (15 min)
Ask: "What did you actually build or finish in the last 90 days?"

Force specificity. Shipped products. Relationships formed. Skills acquired. Revenue moved.
No abstractions. Not "I worked on X" — did you complete it? Ship it? Change something with it?

Then: "What did you avoid?" Same specificity. Not "I procrastinated" — on what, exactly?

Let silence exist. The things left unsaid here are often the most important part of the audit.

---

### 2. What Surprised You (10 min)
Ask: "What happened that you didn't expect — good or bad?"

Surprises reveal where your model of reality was wrong. They are the most valuable data
in the retrospective, because they are the places where your assumptions failed.

Then: "What does that tell you about how you think about [work / relationships / yourself]?"

---

### 3. The Theory (10 min)
Ask: "Why did the quarter go the way it did?"

This will contain rationalizations. Let the first answer land, then press:
- "Is that actually the reason, or is it the comfortable story?"
- "What would have to be different for that outcome not to repeat?"

The goal is a theory they actually believe, not a polished retrospective summary.

---

### 4. The Letter to Past You (10 min)
Ask: "What would you tell yourself 90 days ago that you didn't know then?"

This surfaces the real learning — not what happened, but what they now understand that
they did not understand before. The shift in understanding, not the events, is the point.

Say it out loud or write it. Do not skip this step. The act of articulating the letter
is the work.

---

### 5. The 90-Day Anchor (15 min)
Ask: "What does success look like 90 days from now — in one sentence?"

Then test it:
- "Is that under your control?"
- "Is that what you actually want, or what you think you should want?"
- "What would you have to stop doing to make room for it?"

Close with one specific commitment for the first week. Quarters start with one action,
not a plan.

---

## Post-Session

Archive the full notes in `topics/<name>/sessions/YYYY-MM-DD.md or context/leadership.md`.

Update `context/leadership.md` with a one-line quarterly summary under Historical.

Distill any pattern shifts or major insights to `context/insights/from-executive.md` —
this is the most likely session to produce cross-skill insight worth sharing.
