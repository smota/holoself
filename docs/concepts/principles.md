# Product principles

1. **One whole person, many lenses.** Lenses govern relevance and disclosure, not identity.
2. **Projects keep their artifacts.** Holoself does not centralize execution work.
3. **Self keeps approved reusable knowledge.** Durable facts and stories require review.
4. **Read by default; propose before writing.** Linked access is read-only.
5. **Markdown remains source of truth.** Databases and packets are acceleration or delivery layers.
6. **Generated data is disposable.** Indexes must be safe to rebuild.
7. **Provenance travels with context.** Sources stay visible.
8. **Privacy fails closed.** Unknown options, malformed configuration, invalid visibility, and unsafe paths are rejected.
